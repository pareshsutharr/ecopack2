What changed, exactly

Until September 2025, corrugated boxes carried 12% GST. The 56th GST Council moved corrugated cartons, boxes and cases (HSN 4819) to 5%, effective 22 September 2025 — a direct 7-point relief on packaging spend that many old rate cards and standing POs still miss. Re-check any supplier quotation issued before that date.

HSN in practice

Use HSN 4819 (commonly 48191010 for corrugated cartons/boxes) on invoices and e-way bills. Mixed supplies — say, corrugated shippers plus non-corrugated mono cartons — must be classified line-by-line, because folding cartons of non-corrugated board sit in a different rate bracket.

What it does to your landed cost

On a Rs 10 lakh monthly box spend, the cut releases Rs 70,000/month of tax cash-flow versus the old rate. Combined with kraft paper price movement (~6% upward through 2025), net box pricing still landed favourably for most buyers into 2026.
